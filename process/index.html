Process

How I make these digital things

My process is grounded in the Double Diamond and run with an agile rhythm, but I treat it as a frame, not a script. Every project is different, so I adapt the depth of each phase to the problem, the team, and the constraints. One thing stays constant: from the very start I keep the development language and tech stack in mind, so I design within their real limits and reduce trade offs down the line.

01 · Discover · divergence

Open up the problem

I widen the problem: talking to stakeholders, developers, and clients, and mapping the business goal and the real user need before committing to a direction. Here I lean on AI to speed up research and synthesis, so I reach the signal sooner.

In Thinknube.AI this meant understanding why teams distrust data only research tools before designing the AI conversational flow.

+
IMAGE 2 · research notes, stakeholder map, or discovery board
02 · Define · convergence

Narrow to one direction

I narrow everything down to a clear problem, the core flows, and what ships first. I use AI for strategy and planning: framing scope, structuring flows, and pressure testing priorities. Already at this stage I factor in the tech stack and development language, so design decisions stay feasible and do not create debt later.

In Brikka this was ordering a genuinely complex domain, real estate tokenization, into an investment journey a non crypto user could follow.

03 · Develop · divergence

Ideation and craft

Wireframes, design systems, high fidelity UI, and interactive prototypes, iterated in short agile loops alongside development and product. I prototype fast with AI where it fits, and sometimes build with AI too, creating complex flows that occasionally include databases.

In FanRaise this included the brand identity and a gamified, prediction based donation experience across web and mobile.

04 · Deliver · convergence

Validate, refine, ship

I run lightweight usability testing with Maze for quick unmoderated checks on the key flows, fix what confuses people, and hand off cleanly for build, through to a functional MVP ready to launch.

Throughout

AI as a collaborator

A distinctive part of how I work: I use AI across the whole process, for content, strategy, and planning, to prototype fast and, when it fits, to build complex flows. The design judgment and the craft stay human.
